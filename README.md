# applied_research
This folder is for various programs that are focused on Industrial Engineering and that offers reduced time for completion of the Applied Research (AR) projects 

AR is not a new one;however, it finds it importance this time as there is a dire need of research ready employees; however, there are not many who hold a PhD. AR can help this by addressing the talent shortage by way of improving the supply chain time from say, 5-7 years to 2-3 years. We offer advise and tools that can help bridge the gaps and offer corporates to employ research-skills ready candidates. These can be personalised based on the needs of the business needs, priorities and urgency. We focus on AR based research tasks for emerging technologies 

We have tie-ups with various Corporates and Universities that offer similar programs and we can help you started on these.
